# 📊 Customer Churn Analysis using Python

> An end-to-end customer churn analytics project that analyzes customer behavior, identifies churn drivers, and provides data-driven business recommendations to improve customer retention.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Customer retention has become one of the biggest challenges for subscription-based businesses. This project analyzes customer demographics, subscription details, service usage, and billing information to understand why customers churn and identify customers who are at high risk of leaving.

The project demonstrates the complete analytics lifecycle—from data cleaning and exploratory data analysis to feature engineering and predictive modeling—using Python.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Customer churn negatively impacts revenue, profitability, and long-term customer lifetime value. Businesses need to understand:

* Which customers are likely to churn?
* What factors contribute most to churn?
* Which customer segments should be prioritized for retention?

This project answers these questions through data analysis and machine learning.

---

## 📈 Key Insights

* Customers with month-to-month contracts exhibit the highest churn rate.
* Customers with shorter tenure are significantly more likely to churn.
* Higher monthly charges are associated with increased churn.
* Customers using electronic check payments show relatively higher churn behavior.
* Long-term contracts contribute to better customer retention.
* Customer tenure is one of the strongest predictors of churn.

---

## 💼 Business Recommendations

* Encourage customers to switch from monthly to long-term contracts.
* Offer personalized retention campaigns for high-risk customers.
* Improve onboarding and customer support during the early stages of the customer journey.
* Reward loyal customers through long-term loyalty programs.
* Use predictive analytics to proactively identify and engage customers likely to churn.
